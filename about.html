About Firefighters Together

What this site is

Firefighters Together is a free directory. It helps active and retired firefighters, and their families, find local groups, volunteer roles, family support and official benefit offices in their own state.

Many people leave the job and lose touch. Some never knew a retiree group existed near them. This site exists to make those groups easier to find.

A prototype, unofficial and independent

This is a prototype. We are testing it with real firefighters and families to learn what works and what does not. The listings are a starting point, and some states have more than others.

The site is unofficial and independent. No fire department, union, association or government agency runs it.

Where the listings come from

Listings are compiled from publicly available information. Each entry links to the organization’s own official website. We list organizations only, never individuals.

No organization listed here endorses this site or is affiliated with it.

No advice

This site does not give legal, financial or medical advice. In Station 4, we point to official offices and nothing more. We do not explain the rules or tell anyone whether they qualify. Please confirm all details directly with each office.

How information is checked

Every entry shows a “Last verified” date. That is the day someone last confirmed that the organization’s official website was active and that the listing was accurate. Things change. If a date is old, check with the organization.

Each listing also carries a label. “Verified” means we opened the organization’s own website and checked it. “From official directory” means the link came from an official directory, such as the one kept by the National Association of State Fire Marshals or the U.S. Department of Labor. Listings we have not yet checked are not shown.

Privacy

This site does not track you. It sets no cookies, has no forms and collects no data. It uses no analytics, no ads and no outside scripts. It never asks where you are. You choose your state from a list.

When you follow a link to an organization’s website, that site’s own privacy rules apply.
